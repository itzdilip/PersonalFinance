# Installation Guide: Expense Tracker PWA

As a Progressive Web App (PWA), the Expense Tracker can be installed on your device for a native-like experience.

## Windows & macOS (Chrome/Edge)
1. Open the app URL in your browser.
2. Look for the **Install Icon** (a computer with a down arrow) in the address bar.
3. Click **Install**.
4. The app will now appear in your Start Menu or Applications folder.

## Linux
1. Open the app in Chrome or Brave.
2. Click the three-dot menu (top-right).
3. Select **Install Expense Tracker Offline...** or **Save and Share > Install page as app**.

## Android (Chrome)
1. Open the app URL.
2. Tap the three-dot menu.
3. Tap **Add to Home screen**.

## iOS/iPadOS (Safari)
1. Open the app URL.
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an up arrow).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.

---
**Note**: The app requires a modern browser with Service Worker and IndexedDB support (Chrome 40+, Safari 11.1+, Firefox 44+, Edge 17+).
